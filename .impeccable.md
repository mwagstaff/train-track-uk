## Design Context

### Users
UK rail passengers checking departures, delays, platforms, and journey progress while travelling. Interfaces—especially Live Activities—need to be understandable in a brief glance and in distracting, time-sensitive contexts.

### Brand Personality
Confident, refined, and purposeful. The experience should feel recognisably British-rail-inspired without becoming nostalgic, decorative, or playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Native Apple interaction and typography with compact, information-dense layouts. Dark surfaces and restrained neutrals keep journey data primary; the app icon's deep blue, vivid blue, red, and white provide controlled brand accents. Avoid generic transport symbols, ornamental graphics, glow effects, and colour treatments that compete with service-status colours.

### Design Principles
- Prioritise glanceability: route, departure time, destination, and platform must scan immediately.
- Use brand colour structurally and sparingly, such as perimeter accents, rather than as decoration.
- Preserve semantic colour: red, amber, and green continue to communicate service status clearly.
- Prefer clean alignment, consistent 4-point spacing, and native controls over added visual elements.
- Support Dynamic Type, dark and light appearances, reduced luminance, and compact presentation sizes.
